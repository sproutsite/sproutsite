# Starter hero and footer backgrounds

Generated on 2026-11-25 with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool. The maintainer's screenshot “Elegante Website mit blauen Wellenverläufen.png” was supplied as a style reference. The resulting images contain only decorative waves. Sharp resized them proportionally to 1920 pixels wide and encoded the hero as JPEG and the footer as WebP. The optimized raster files are the canonical replacements and the starter asset manifest records their checksums.

## Hero prompt

```text
Create an original, background-only digital illustration for the full-width hero and navigation of a starter website. The attached website mockup is a style and composition reference for its TOP banner only. Recreate the calm feeling of a near-black navy field (#06182f to #0b2546) with three very broad, smooth overlapping wave surfaces. One rises toward the far upper right, one sweeps low on the left and curls gently up at right, and a muted deep petrol blue-teal surface emerges at bottom right. Subtle edge lighting and smooth internal gradients, with a restrained soft cobalt-blue highlight left of center. Keep the whole left and center dark enough for white body text, including on a center crop for narrow screens. Very wide panoramic landscape composition, approximately 4.5:1 target crop, main contours within the middle horizontal half of the canvas so a wide crop retains them, full bleed. Elegant website background art, polished matte surfaces. Absolutely no typography, letters, logo, browser UI, icons, outlines, borders, mid-page white sections, objects, texture, grain, or bright neon. Output just the single uninterrupted dark background image.
```

## Footer prompt

```text
Create an original, background-only digital illustration for a full-width website footer. Use the attached mockup's BOTTOM banner as a style reference. Design a near-black midnight navy upper field (#06182f to #0b2546), gently overlapping horizontal wave surfaces below, a shallow valley at center-left and a crest growing toward the right, with the lowest right band fading to restrained dark petrol teal. Thin soft edge lighting, smooth gradients and generous dark areas for white links. Match its dark elegant navy-blue atmosphere and continuous softly shaded waves. Very wide panoramic landscape for a 6:1 website strip. Keep the main wave edges within the central horizontal third of the composition so the crop retains the curves; render dark background across the full image. No text, icons, logo, website UI, mockup frame, borders, white areas, objects, grain, or bright neon. Output just the uninterrupted footer background image.
```
