## Make the first change

Open `src/content/pages/hero.md`. Change the value of `title` to your own heading, for example `Welcome to my website`. Replace the introduction below the closing `---` with a short sentence about what visitors can find here. Keep the other fields and both `---` lines.

Save the file and return to the browser. The new heading and introduction should appear in the local preview. You have made your first website change.

To change regular body text, open `src/content/pages/01-introduction.md`. More examples follow under [pages and sections](/documentation/getting-started/your-website/pages-and-sections/).
