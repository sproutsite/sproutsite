## A single badge

Use `badges` to add a status or category label to a Card. A single badge needs an object with `text`; `variant` and `style` match the Badge component. Labels keep a 1 rem inset from the image edge.

<Tabs label="A single badge: Preview and code" variant="border">
    <Tab title="Preview">
<Card image={cardImage} imageAlt="Three-dimensional illustration of modular content cards" badges={{text: 'Partly available', variant: 'info', style: 'soft'}}>

### A clear status

The badge belongs to the image area and stays at its bottom right, independently of the card body.

</Card>
