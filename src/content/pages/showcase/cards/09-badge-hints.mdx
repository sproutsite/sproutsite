## Hints on hover and keyboard focus

Optionally add `hover` to a badge descriptor with a short, non-empty hint. It appears on pointer hover and keyboard focus, both on images and on cards without an image.

Tab reaches badges that have a hint, with a visible focus indicator. Escape closes the hint without moving focus. You can move the pointer onto the hint to continue reading it. Without `hover`, the badge remains a normal label without an extra tab stop.

Hints contain plain text, not links or other controls. Keep essential information in the card body.
