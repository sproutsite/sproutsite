## Lifted

The active tab is visually raised above the others. The tab list scrolls horizontally when space is limited.

<Tabs label="Lifted: Preview and code" variant="border">
    <Tab title="Preview">
        <Tabs label="Presentation and interaction" variant="lift">
            <Tab title="Desktop and mobile">
                ### Room for content

                Panels use the available width. Long tab lists scroll inside the component without widening the page.
            </Tab>
            <Tab title="Keyboard interaction">
                ### Switch without a mouse

                Arrow keys select the neighboring tab, wrapping from the last back to the first. Tab then moves focus into the active panel and its links.
            </Tab>
            <Tab title="Markdown and links">
                ### Content as usual
