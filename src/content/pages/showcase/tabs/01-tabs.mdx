## Tabs

Tabs switch between related content panels. Each example has its own preview/code group: Preview opens first, and Code provides the complete MDX example and import. Try all three styles. Each group works independently and its content remains Markdown. Press Tab to reach the selected tab, use Left and Right to switch tabs, and Home or End to reach the first or last tab.

**Atomic Design:** Molecule `Tabs` with the atom `Tab` for each content panel.

Component paths within `src/themes/default/components/`: `molecules/Tabs/` and `atoms/Tab/`.

### Lifted

The active tab is visually raised above the others. The tab list scrolls horizontally when space is limited.
