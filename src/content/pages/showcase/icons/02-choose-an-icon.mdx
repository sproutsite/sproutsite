## Choose an icon

Find a symbol in the [Lucide catalog](https://lucide.dev/icons/). Use its canonical Astro component name in PascalCase: `arrow-right` becomes `ArrowRight`, `file-text` becomes `FileText`, and `sprout` becomes `Sprout`. An unknown name stops the build with an error.
