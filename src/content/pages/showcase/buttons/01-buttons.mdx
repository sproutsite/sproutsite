## Buttons

Buttons emphasize an action or a navigation link. With `href`, `Button` renders a semantic `a` element for navigation; without it, it renders a native `button`. Sproutsite types the color, size, and style props; DaisyUI provides their presentation.

**Atomic Design:** Atom `Button`.

Component path within `src/themes/default/components/`: `atoms/Button/`.
