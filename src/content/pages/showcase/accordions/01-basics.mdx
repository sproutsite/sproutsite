## Plain questions and answers

`Accordion` groups direct `AccordionItem` children. Each item needs a `title`; Markdown in its default slot becomes the answer. The default opens one item at a time. Closed items have a white surface and gray border; open items use a light blue tint and blue border. The indicator changes from Plus to ChevronUp.

**Atomic Design:** Both components are molecules in `components/molecules/Accordion/` and `components/molecules/AccordionItem/`. They reuse the Icon atom and DaisyUI's collapse styles with native details and summary elements.
