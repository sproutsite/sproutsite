## Custom backgrounds

`backgroundColor` belongs to the icon box. `surfaceColor` sets the closed item background; `openSurfaceColor` and `openBorderColor` control the open state. These three surface props accept CSS hex colors. Omit them for the blue-and-white defaults. When changing surfaces, check text and focus contrast yourself.
