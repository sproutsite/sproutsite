## Multiple answers and rich content

Use `multiple` for setup instructions or reference notes that readers compare side by side. Several `open` items are allowed in this mode. Separate groups never close one another's items.

The whole closed box opens on click. When open, click the heading or noninteractive answer area to close it. Links, buttons, and text selection remain usable without closing. Tab focuses each heading; Enter and Space toggle it. A small processed TypeScript script adds whole-box closing and independent single-open groups. Without JavaScript, native details still open and close through their headings, but groups allow multiple open items.
