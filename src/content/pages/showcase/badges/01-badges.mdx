## Badges

Badges identify short status values, categories, or counts. They supplement content rather than replace an explanation. Important information still needs a clear description in the surrounding text.

**Atomic Design:** Atom `Badge`.

Component path within `src/themes/default/components/`: `atoms/Badge/`.
