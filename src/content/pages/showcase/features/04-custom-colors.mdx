## Hex colors

`color` also accepts CSS hex values with 3, 4, 6, or 8 hexadecimal digits, such as `#527b37` or `#527b37cc`. A hex value sets the icon color and a softly tinted background. In this mode, `variant` is ignored: `soft`, `solid`, and `plain` produce the same appearance. Choose a color that remains visible against the surrounding section.
