## Contact by email

If you write to <LegalIdentity part="email" />, your sender address, message content and information supplied with the email are processed to respond to your inquiry. Contacting us is voluntary. A response requires a reachable sender address.

The controller personally operates and manages the mailbox on the same server as the website. No additional third-party mailbox service is used; the hosting infrastructure identified above also supports this mail server.

General project inquiries are handled under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is responding to questions and concerns about the project. If an inquiry concerns entering into or performing a contract, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR applies.

### Message retention

General project inquiries are deleted once they have been fully handled and no further clarification is needed, and in any event **no later than <PrivacyInfrastructure part="email-retention" /> months after completion**. Messages are kept longer only where required by law or necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Any further processing is then limited to that purpose.

This period is a chosen upper limit for ordinary inquiries, not a universal statutory retention period for all emails.

The deletion rule applies to project correspondence in the active mailbox. Retention of any external copies and backups has not yet been conclusively established, so the same deadline cannot be promised for them.
