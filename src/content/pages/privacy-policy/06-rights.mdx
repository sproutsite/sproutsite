## Access, correction and deletion

Subject to the applicable legal conditions, your rights include:

- Access to your personal data under Article 15 GDPR;
- Correction of inaccurate or incomplete data under Article 16 GDPR;
- Erasure under Article 17 and restriction of processing under Article 18 GDPR;
- Data portability under Article 20 GDPR where processing is based on consent or a contract and carried out by automated means.

**Objection:** Where processing relies on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object on grounds relating to your particular situation under Article 21(1) GDPR. Processing will then continue only under the conditions specified in that provision.
